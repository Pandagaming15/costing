4. Formula and step-by-step solution

A. Total cost of each ingredient

Ingredient cost = Quantity × Price per unit

Enter ingredients to see the calculation for each row.

B. Total direct cost

Total Direct Cost = Add all ingredient costs

C. Total production cost

Total Production Cost = Total Direct Cost + Indirect Cost

D. Cost per serving / unit cost

Unit Cost = Total Production Cost ÷ Number of servings

E. Selling price with mark-up

Selling Price = Unit Cost × (1 + Mark-up percentage ÷ 100)

Note: This calculator uses the standard total-cost method. If your instructor requires a different indirect-cost formula, follow the formula in your module.
