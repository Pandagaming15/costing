2. Compute the Unit Cost

The unit cost is the cost incurred to sell the cooked product. Divide the cumulative expenses by the number of servings or yield produced.

Module formula:
Unit Cost = Ingredient Cost + ½ of cost of ingredientsNo. of yields or servings
=₱198.50 + ₱99.2510 servings
=₱297.7510
= ₱29.775 per serving

3. Pricing the Product

Apply the mark-up to the unit cost to calculate the selling price per serving. Since the unit cost is already per serving, do not divide by the number of servings again.

Module worked-example method:
Selling Price per Serving = Unit Cost × (1 + Mark-up Rate)
= ₱29.775 × (1 + 0.25)
= ₱29.775 × 1.25
= ₱37.21875
= ₱37.22 per serving

4. Compute the Profit

Multiply the selling price per serving by the number of servings to find total sales. Then subtract the total production cost to find the profit earned from the whole batch.

Profit formula:
Total Profit = (Selling Price per Serving × Number of Servings) − Total Production Cost
= ₱37.22 × 10 = ₱372.20 total sales
= ₱372.20 − ₱297.75
= ₱74.45 total profit for the batch
Profit per Serving = Total Profit ÷ Number of Servings = ₱7.445
